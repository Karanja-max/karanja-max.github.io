Case study · Client work · MK Agency

Baple Gadgets

An online store selling gadgets, including iPhones. I designed how its products are ranked and built its checkout data flow.

Role
Lead developer
Client
Baple Gadgets
Year
2026
Stack
Next.js, NestJS, Prisma

The problem

The store needed to show products in an order its owner chooses, not only newest first, and its checkout needed to move order data between the storefront and the backend.

What I built

  • A custom database schema in Prisma that supports non-chronological product ranking.
  • The dynamic checkout data flow, using Next.js on the storefront and NestJS on the backend.

How ranking works

Products are ranked through a hierarchical schema defined in Prisma, not by sorting on the date they were added.
